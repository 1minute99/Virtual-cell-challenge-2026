## Figure 1. Decomposition and zero-shot setting

The pseudobulk response of target t in context c is decomposed as δ_{c,t} = μ + a_t + b_c + i_{c,t} + ε_{c,t}
(after Molina & Zhang). μ and a_t can be transferred from source contexts. b_c and i_{c,t} are specific to the context
and, for an unseen context c*, must be predicted from its control-cell descriptor x_{c*} by a map learned on C source
contexts. ε is removed by split-half estimation. Each research question is placed where it acts. RQ1 compares methods
with ceilings built from μ + a + b (registered r_max, the oracle, and the measurement ceiling). RQ2 asks how many
contexts the learned map needs: C, not the number of cells or targets, is its sample size, and at most C − 1 descriptor
directions are identifiable (Proposition 1). RQ3 asks whether the residual of the prediction lies on c*'s own
control PCs. Schematic only; no data.

## Figure 2. Tahoe: harm and break-even of the learned context pathway

**(a)** Primary quantity (amendment A1): the context-pathway gain, defined as residual MSE of the constant-z ablation
minus residual MSE of the context model, plotted against the number of training contexts C (log axis;
C ∈ {6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40}). The thick line is the run mean, i.e. the unweighted mean over the 8 held-out contexts
of each run, then over 3 seeds × 6 drug folds (18 runs, 1,008 context-level rows). The band is a 95 % cluster-bootstrap
CI (B = 2,000; runs resampled, then held-out contexts within runs; seed 20261107). Thin lines are the three per-seed
means. The black diamond and bar show C*_emp = 37.4 [33.3, > 40], the first upward zero crossing, interpolated
linearly in ln C. The upper CI bound is censored: 8.75 % of bootstrap draws do not cross by C = 40. The gain at C = 12
is −4.20 [−5.15, −3.32] × 10⁻⁴, so H2a is confirmed. Arrows mark the Proposition-2 break-evens, which lie beyond the
axis: C*_min = 80.6 [68.5, 97.6] and C*_theory = 230 [192, 287]. Both were estimated blind to the curve (B = 2,000 over
drugs and split seeds; 185 drugs × 48 lines). C*_theory / C*_emp = 6.2 falls outside [0.5, 2], so H2b and the
quantitative Proposition 2 are rejected. **(b)** The same runs with other quantities. Orange: the registered
quantity, MSE gain over the analytic zero residual. It is confounded, because under the frozen selection rule the
network cannot select step 0 (C* 37.7, also "confirmed"). Green, **exploratory**: the include-zero rerun, in which the
step-0 (zero-residual) checkpoint may be selected (C* 36.2). Here the harm at small C mostly disappears because the
network falls back to zero. Bands as in (a).

## Figure 4. Methods on the ceiling scale (RQ1)

Noise-corrected Pearson r with the destination response for each harness panel (n targets: H1 300, HCT116 232, KOLF
278, RN panels 300; 6,009 genes; 5 split seeds; target bootstrap B = 2,000, seed 20261113; all quantities share the same
draws). Upper lane, ceilings: the measurement ceiling (black tick), the registered r_max = √[(Var a + Var b) / Var_rep]
(open diamond), the oracle μ + a_t + b_c (black dot, a diagnostic and not a zero-shot method), and r_proj, the
best-rescaled oracle (open grey square, **exploratory**). Lower lane, zero-shot methods: the frozen pipeline
M1 + M2 + M3 (blue) and the plain cross-line mean (orange; its source-noise overlap is corrected). On H1 the oracle
exceeds the registered r_max (0.490 vs 0.423; paired difference CI [+0.016, +0.114]). The registered ceiling estimator
is therefore internally inconsistent, and H1 is **untestable as registered**; the pipeline's r 0.380 [0.348, 0.411]
would be a fraction of 0.899 of it. On HCT116 (OR), r_max and the oracle are not estimable. On the other panels r_max is
the registered estimator applied as a secondary analysis. It falls below the oracle on HepG2 and RPE1 and exceeds the
measurement ceiling on K562 and Jurkat, which shows that it is ill-posed per panel (exploratory reading). The
architecture predictions are not on disk and are not shown.

## Figure 5. RQ3: residual energy on the destination's control subspace and the M3 correction

**(a)** Fraction F(U) of the transfer residual's energy (truth − pre-M3 prediction, split-half noise control, G-cap
6,014 genes) captured by four 8-dimensional subspaces (log axis): the destination's top-8 control PCs; other harness
lines' top-8 control PCs (mean over 6 lines); PCs of gene-wise permuted destination controls (mean over permutations);
and a random (Haar) subspace (≈ k/G). The CIs are target-bootstrap 95 % CIs (B = 2,000, mean over 5 split seeds) and
are often narrower than the markers. **(b)** D_other = F(U_dest) − mean F(U_j). Filled blue: the registered design,
where U_dest comes from one control third and U_j from all of line j's controls. H3a is **confirmed** on H1
(D_other 0.018 [0.009, 0.027]; D_perm 0.078 [0.065, 0.092]; negative control ≤ 1.8 % of the observed D). Open orange,
**exploratory**: the same contrast at matched control-cell numbers (5 reps). It is positive on 7/7 panels, which shows
that the registered negatives on K562 and Jurkat are a sample-size artefact (H1: 0.009 [0.0003, 0.018]). **(c)** H3b:
ΔPDS of M3 (destination-PC deflation) against each of the five alternatives. Blue: 7-panel mean with a 95 % bootstrap
CI (B = 2,000). Grey dots: the 6 non-H1 panels. Black open circle: H1 offline. Orange diamond: H1 scored with the real
cell-eval2 scorer, available for "no correction" and Systema-best. All five means are > 0 with CIs excluding 0, but on
H1 the real scorer favours Systema-style removal (−0.0076). The rule requires the same sign against all five, so H3b
is **inconclusive**.
