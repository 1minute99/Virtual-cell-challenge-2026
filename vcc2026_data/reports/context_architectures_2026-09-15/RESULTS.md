# Eight context-dependent residual architectures — completed Jiang evaluation

**No architecture establishes reliable transferable context information in this small sweep.** Three have tiny positive primary gains over b16, but the matched controls, independent cell-line consistency and shuffle diagnostics prevent a success claim. All eight requested architectures and 184 outer model-folds completed, including 3,300 cached training fits, in about 7.4 minutes for the main run. ## Protocol and architecture details

The eight modules use one `prepare → fit → predict` interface in `scripts/context_architectures.py` and one nested runner, `scripts/run_context_architectures.py`. The existing `jiang_benchmark.load_panel`, PDS, and metrics are unchanged. Raw integer counts are normalized over the complete library to log1p(CP10K), replicate controls are matched, the same min-25-cell balanced target panels and official-gene intersections are retained, and every screened target-gene coordinate is excluded.

The official b16 checkpoint is untouched. On Jiang, each allowed source set has the **same pre-existing rank-128, alpha-1, strength-1 denoiser**, fixed for every architecture, ablation and seed. Its overall reference is 0.7790152445958903; mean transfer is 0.7367381618185269. Both exactly reproduce the prior benchmark. No b16 strength or preprocessing setting was tuned here.

Response bases use 8 or 16 components; control PCA uses at most two components, as in the prior benchmark. All bases/scales use permitted training lines only. The module parameter column excludes the common data-derived gene bases and frozen denoiser. No target or cell-line ID embedding is used. For each training destination, a common full-source and reduced-source episode are built without that destination. Both biological replicate labels are retained. Mean-squared error averages them with equal weight; cells/replicates do not count as additional independent cell lines.

Every output is a signed low-rank residual added to the frozen baseline, centered across targets to prevent a generic all-target offset. Training uses 160 full-batch AdamW steps, learning rate 0.01, weight decay 0.001 and residual-energy penalty 0.5. The small sweep has two latent ranks and output strengths {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1}; strength zero is an explicit fallback. Only inner whole-line PDS selects settings. Same seed and splits are used across families. Selected models and matched no-context versions are retrained at seeds 1 and 2 without outer retuning.

Pairwise DeltaNet additionally learns each ordered source-to-destination response difference (auxiliary weight 0.5); the average predicted difference is anchored on b16. The hypernetwork has a rank-4 adapter with a tiny nonlinear context modulator. DeepSets conditions each source encoder on source and destination-difference context; CNP queries a pooled context-response representation after aggregation and also predicts diagonal variance. MoE uses three tiny linear experts, context routing and load/entropy regularization. Tensor uses rank-4 CP-style factors with a linear untreated-expression cell factor. The metric uses a perturbation-dependent positive diagonal metric over learned context directions; its weighted-source innovation is relative to uniform transfer. The exact GP has three shared learned kernel/noise parameters and only a few source contexts per solve; it predicts context-dependent deviations around the source mean, added to b16.

The no-context ablation removes both source and destination context from the same module. We report both independently inner-selected and rank/strength-matched versions. Wrong context is the average of scores after substituting **each** allowed source's context for the destination, leaving response inputs fixed. A zero-query-context control is separate. One fixed training-residual-label derangement preserves the two replicate labels' pairing, and one fixed training-context permutation breaks context/response correspondence. Both retain the frozen baseline and source responses, so their full-model scores are not expected to be chance. Thirty-two prediction-target derangements provide the actual target-information floor for each prediction.

PDS, replicate-specific PDS, centered PDS, cosine, noise-corrected centered cosine, and common-response energy are inherited unchanged. Added diagnostics are row-wise Pearson, RMSE/MAE, relative L2 error, absolute log response-magnitude ratio, and residual magnitude relative to b16. GP/CNP latent predictive variances are saved, with unshrunk-model NLL and 90% coverage diagnostics. These estimates omit uncertainty in the frozen baseline and gene directions outside the latent basis; selected correction variance is saved separately from unscaled residual predictive variance.

## Context evidence, replication and stability

The six cell lines are the independent biological units. We average stimuli within each line and then weight lines equally; the 23 stimulus-line folds are not 23 independent biologies. The seed-0 nested result is the primary table; repeated seeds assess stability rather than selecting the best seed. **GP initialization and optimization are deterministic, so its identical seed repeats are not independent stability evidence.** Exact paired sign-flip diagnostics over six lines and Holm adjustment across eight models are in `model_comparison.csv`; these are exploratory diagnostics on an already studied benchmark, not a pristine confirmatory test. A flexible residual that wins without correct context does not establish transferable context learning. The mean per-fold above-shuffle ratio and the ratio of equal-cell-line means are both saved and intentionally distinguished.

## Failure modes and recommendations

The largest primary PDS gain, Pairwise DeltaNet's +0.000267, is smaller than its 0.000470 seed standard deviation. Its mean across three seeds is 0.778898, below b16. Removing context beats it on every aggregate seed comparison, and both separate-replicate PDS averages are below b16. The K562 gain (+0.006677) is offset by the MCF7 loss (−0.007432). This is a useful direct-change architecture but it has not demonstrated transferable destination context.

DeepSets and CNP fit their training residuals substantially, yet inner selection disables their corrections in 16/23 and 13/23 folds. At strength one the context DeepSets score is only 0.732062, versus 0.779272 for its matched no-context variant. Residual shrinkage prevents much of this deterioration; more output strength is not the solution. Target centering makes the maximum observed generic-response energy negligible, so the earlier all-target-offset shortcut is not driving these results.

MoE routing did not collapse to one expert: the average held-out routing distribution uses about 2.45 effective experts out of three, with mean maximum weight about 0.57. Nevertheless correct context is worse than the matched no-context version. Its independently selected no-context variant scores 0.782063, the strongest context-free result here; this is a possible denoising lead, not evidence for the present context hypothesis. Tensor loses to its matched no-context version in every line average.

The two architectures worth a limited next round **for the context hypothesis** are:

1. **Perturbation-conditioned learned metric transfer.** Only 44–76 module parameters; +0.000244 over b16, positive across all three aggregate training seeds, and both replicate averages improve slightly. It also beats the label-shuffled model. However it wins only 3/6 lines and the zero-query-context control (0.779441) beats the correct query (0.779259). Mean source-weight variation across targets is small (about 0.0103), so useful perturbation-specific geometry is not established. Next compare this learned metric against a perturbation-independent metric of matched capacity, expand training-label/context permutations, and tune only a small metric-rank/regularization grid in inner folds.

2. **Shared-kernel GP as the minimal context model and comparator.** Three learned parameters; +0.000221 over b16, 4/6 line wins and correct query better than wrong queries in all six line averages. Yet label-shuffled and context-shuffled training score about 0.779511, above the correctly trained GP's 0.779236. This prevents attributing the tiny gain to supervised context learning. Next compare a fixed, untrained kernel with learned kernels under identical inner selection, test conservative noise/length-scale regularization, and calibrate uncertainty on inner replicate folds. Its deterministic seed repeats do not establish robustness.

These are **exploratory tuning priorities, not promoted submission candidates**. Continue only if correct destination context improves over b16, matched no-context, zero/wrong-query and training-shuffle controls with consistent cell-line and replicate gains. Then freeze the chosen model and test transfer on broader held-out biologies before official-validation count reconstruction. Keep b16 as the current official reference.

CNP and GP saved latent uncertainty. Their nominal 90% unshrunk residual intervals cover about 88.3% and 98.5% of held-out latent replicate values respectively; GP is very conservative in this aggregate diagnostic. These are approximate marginal uncertainties conditional on fitted features/source summaries, and omit baseline and omitted-gene uncertainty. They are not validated confidence intervals for the full submission.

## Verification

Seven core scientific tests cover all eight modules: frozen-baseline parity and separate replicate labels; held-out-response poisoning; no-context invariance; permutation/variable-source behavior; serialization and positive uncertainty; label-shuffle pairing; fast-PDS parity with full gene-space scoring. An additional test poisons the entire outer fold and checks all inner scores and selections for all eight families, with fresh fits and no checkpoint cache. The existing PDS routine separately matches the installed cell-eval2 scorer on random, zero, constant and tied predictions. Real IFNB/A549 nested poisoning and source/checkpoint hashes are audited separately in `real_outer_poisoning.json` and `verification.json`.
